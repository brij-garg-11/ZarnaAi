## Businesses (SMB)

Features available to small business owners (e.g. comedy clubs, venues) who sign up as a business account.

---

### AI-Powered SMS Conversations

**Conversational AI replies**  
When a customer texts in, the bot replies using the business's knowledge base and live calendar data. Replies are generated by Gemini (primary), with OpenAI and Anthropic as fallbacks.

**Knowledge base (FAQ)**  
A static set of facts about the business — hours, location, policies, events — that the AI uses to answer customer questions accurately.

**Live calendar scraping**  
The bot pulls the venue's current show/event schedule from the website in real time and uses it to answer "what's on this weekend?" style questions. Results are cached for 2 hours to avoid hammering the site.

**STOP / opt-out handling**  
If a customer texts STOP, their status is immediately set to `stopped` in the database. They are excluded from all future AI replies and blast messages. Fully TCPA-compliant at the application layer.

---

### New Customer Onboarding

**Opt-in detection**  
When a new number texts in for the first time, the AI determines whether the message is an opt-in signal (YES, sure, I'm in, their specific keyword, etc.) or just a question. Only confirmed opt-ins are added as subscribers.

**Welcome message + preference question**  
On a confirmed first opt-in, the bot sends a welcome message followed by a preference question (e.g. "What brings you in most — live shows, open mics, or just dinner?"). The answer is stored and used to personalize future outreach.

**A2P compliance line**  
Every new subscriber's first message automatically includes "Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out." appended, satisfying CTIA carrier requirements.

**Contact card (vCard MMS)**  
On first opt-in, the bot can optionally send a vCard so the business saves as a contact on the customer's phone. Controlled via the My Bot toggle — now reads from the database so the toggle actually works.

**Outreach invite claim**  
If the business ran an outreach campaign with invite codes (e.g. "first 50 people get a free ticket"), a customer texting in with the matching keyword can claim it automatically. The system generates and assigns a ticket number.

---

### Engagement & Audience Intelligence

**Geo tagging**  
Phone area codes are used to infer location on first contact. Customers are tagged by region automatically — no action required from the owner.

**Intent preference tagging**  
Based on the answers to the preference question and ongoing conversations, the system tags customers by interest (e.g. `SHOW_SEEKERS`, `DEAL_SEEKERS`) and stores those tags for targeting.

**Engagement scoring**  
Every customer gets a 0–100 engagement score based on how recently they've replied, how many messages they've sent, and how long they've been a subscriber. Updated automatically on a nightly schedule.

**Fan tiers (Superfan / Engaged / Lurker / Dormant)**  
Customers are automatically bucketed into tiers based on their engagement score. Used for blast targeting and audience breakdowns.

---

### Blast Messaging

**Compose and send blasts**  
Write a message, pick an audience, send immediately or schedule for later. The blast runs asynchronously in the background so the UI stays responsive.

**Audience targeting**  
Blasts can be sent to: everyone, a specific tag (e.g. SHOW_SEEKERS), a geographic area, customers who attended a specific show, a fan tier (Superfan, Engaged, etc.), a random percentage of the list, compound AND filters combining multiple segments, or the top-N most engaged customers.

**Scheduled sends**  
Pick a future date and time. The blast is queued and fires automatically. The scheduler checks every 60 seconds and claims the job atomically — no double-sends even with multiple server workers. Now correctly interprets your local timezone (not UTC) when scheduling.

**Mid-send cancellation**  
If a blast is running and you need to stop it, you can cancel it. The worker checks for cancellation between recipients and halts cleanly.

**Per-recipient tracked links**  
Any link in a blast message is automatically rewritten to a tracked `/t/` URL. You can see which customers actually clicked. Each link is unique per recipient.

**Credit-gated sends**  
Blasts check your remaining credits before and during sending (every 50 recipients). If you run out mid-blast, it stops rather than letting you go over.

**Quiz session start via blast**  
A blast can kick off an interactive quiz. When the blast goes out, the next inbound message from each recipient is intercepted by the quiz engine instead of the normal AI.

## Performers (Creators)

Features available to performers, comedians, and creators (like Zarna Garg) running an AI SMS fan engagement bot.

---

### AI Fan Conversations

**Full AI pipeline (ZarnaBrain)**  
Every inbound fan message goes through: intent classification → complexity routing → RAG retrieval → LLM reply generation → tone adjustment → emphasis throttling → response trimming. This is the core of the product.

**Intent classification**  
The AI figures out what the fan is asking about before generating a reply — ticket links, merch, podcast, shows, booking, general conversation, or a greeting. Fast keyword heuristics run first; Gemini handles anything ambiguous.

**Complexity routing**  
Simpler messages get a faster, cheaper model. Complex, emotionally rich, or high-tier fan messages get routed to a more capable model (Anthropic → OpenAI → Gemini fallback chain).

**RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**  
The AI searches through the creator's training data (everything known about them — past interviews, show info, social media, bio) to include relevant context in every reply. Zarna's runs from files; future creators use Postgres pgvector.

**Fan memory**  
After every reply, the AI extracts and stores facts about that fan (their name, where they're from, what they care about, their relationship with the creator). Next time they text, the AI already knows them. This runs in the background and doesn't slow replies.

**Conversation history**  
The AI has access to the last 8 turns of the conversation, so replies feel continuous and contextual — not like talking to a fresh chatbot every time.

**Multi-provider LLM fallback**  
If Gemini is down, the bot falls back to OpenAI, then Anthropic. Even structured replies (ticket links, merch info) now have fallbacks — a Gemini outage no longer silences those replies.

**Banned words enforcement**  
Any word on the creator's banned list is caught post-generation. If a reply contains a banned term, the system replaces it with a safe fallback rather than sending it.

**Tone classification**  
The AI reads the emotional register of each fan message and adjusts its tone accordingly — warmer for emotional messages, more playful for banter.

**Emphasis throttling**  
Prevents the bot from using exclamation points, bold language, or hype too frequently — keeps replies feeling natural rather than salesy.

**Response length control**  
Replies are capped at 3 sentences / ~380 characters, appropriate for SMS.

### Live Shows

**Show creation**  
Set up a show with a name, keyword (e.g. "NYC"), a signup window (dates/times), and delivery channel (Twilio or SlickText).

**Keyword signup**  
When a fan texts the keyword during the active window, they are automatically registered and get a join confirmation SMS.

Live show confirmations use a thread pool with a ceiling (20 workers). High-signup moments don't crash the server.

**Blast context injection**  
When there's an active blast running, the AI is aware of it and can reference it naturally in fan replies — the bot knows what you just sent.

**Quiz sessions**  
Start a quiz via a live show blast. Fans who receive the blast are put into quiz mode — their next messages are intercepted and scored by the quiz engine.

### Opt-Out & Compliance

**STOP keyword handling**  
Fan STOP messages are caught before reaching the AI. The carrier sends the mandatory opt-out confirmation. The fan's number is recorded in `broadcast_optouts` and excluded from all future blasts.

**Blast opt-out tracking**  
Both Twilio and SlickText STOP signals are recorded. Fan count and opt-out records are updated in the database.

**Rate limiting**  
Inbound messages are rate-limited to 3 per 60 seconds per phone number. Prevents fans from hammering the bot and running up costs.
