**Built on his knowledge, and only his.** The assistant is trained exclusively on Matthew's own material: his rankings, columns, Love/Hate analysis, and podcast. Every answer reflects his take, in his voice, and it never speaks for anyone else.

**He decides what goes in.** Matthew (or his team) chooses exactly what the assistant draws from and what stays off-limits. If there's a take he doesn't want it to give, it won't. He's in the driver's seat at all times.

**Auto-updated three times a week.** Fantasy moves fast, and a stale assistant is worthless in-season. As Matthew publishes new rankings and analysis, the assistant absorbs it automatically, three times a week, with no manual work on his end.
